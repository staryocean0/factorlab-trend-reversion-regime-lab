## 1. Authoritative product target

The preferred acquisition source is the **China Financial Futures Exchange (CFFEX) official historical data service**.

CFFEX's historical-data service states that its products cover historical market information generated by all listed products/contracts and include snapshot data. The service lists four product families:

1. CFFEX Level-2 historical data;
2. CFFEX Level-1 historical data;
3. one-minute historical database;
4. five-minute historical database.

For the R1_B primary execution identity, the purchase/request target is:

> **CFFEX Level-2 historical snapshot data for CSI1000 index options (`MO`)**

Level-2 is the conservative first-choice acquisition because this research requires executable best bid/ask and size at inherited causal entry/exit clocks. Level-1 may replace Level-2 only if the official field specification or delivered sample is inspected **before option outcomes** and demonstrably contains timestamped `bid1`, `ask1`, `bid1_size`, and `ask1_size` with stable semantics.

The one-minute and five-minute historical databases are not substitutes for the primary study because bar OHLC does not preserve executable spread.

Official acquisition pages:

- CFFEX historical-data service: https://www.cffex.com.cn/lssjfw/
- CFFEX downloads / application materials: https://www.cffex.com.cn/zlxz/
- CFFEX CSI1000 index-option product page: https://www.cffex.com.cn/zz1000gzqq/
- CFFEX index-option trading rules: https://www.cffex.com.cn/cn/ssxz/20221214/43100.html

The downloads page currently lists, under historical-data service:

and under product pricing:

## 2. Instrument scope

Underlying: CSI1000 Index (`000852.SH` in the repository's index data convention).

Option family: CFFEX CSI1000 index option, trading code family `MO`.

Official contract rules establish:

- call code: `MO<contract-month>-C-<strike>`;
- put code: `MO<contract-month>-P-<strike>`;
- multiplier: RMB 100 per index point;
- European exercise;
- cash settlement;
- contract months: current month, next two months, and subsequent three quarterly months;
- continuous trading session: 09:30-11:30 and 13:00-15:00, with opening auction before continuous trading.

Historical acquisition start: **2022-07-22**, the official launch date of CSI1000 index futures/options.

Historical reusable-instrument-development end: **2026-09-10 23:59:59 Asia/Shanghai**.

Prospective validation starts: **2026-09-11 00:00:00 Asia/Shanghai**. A later download of pre-freeze history does not make those historical observations prospective.

## 3. Exact requested market-data content

Request **all listed MO contracts and all available historical quote snapshots** in the requested date range, not only ATM contracts and not only contracts later selected by R1_B events. This prevents event-conditioned acquisition selection.

The delivery must contain, directly or through a stable documented mapping, at least:

- exchange contract code;
- option call/put identity;
- strike;
- contract expiry / last trading date or a stable contract master that supplies it;
- exchange-local quote timestamp;
- best bid price (`bid1`);
- best bid size (`bid1_size`);
- best ask price (`ask1`);
- best ask size (`ask1_size`);
- last price;
- volume;
- open interest;
- trading / quote status or enough source status information to distinguish valid continuous-session quotes from unavailable/invalid states.

If Level-2 supplies deeper book levels, keep them in the raw archive; the frozen primary mapping uses only level 1. Do not discard raw depth merely because the first study does not consume it.

## 4. Required metadata / documentation to obtain with the data

Do not treat bare quote files as admitted. Obtain and preserve, where supplied:

- official product/data dictionary;
- file-layout/version document;
- timestamp precision and timezone semantics;
- snapshot frequency / generation rule;
- zero/missing quote encoding;
- trading-status encoding;
- volume semantics (cumulative vs interval);
- open-interest semantics;
- contract-master / expiry convention;
- correction/republication policy;
- delivery date and source identity;
- purchase/order/application reference that can be retained privately if it contains sensitive account information.

Public repository documents must not expose private account numbers, personal contact details, invoice identifiers, credentials, or non-public download links. The public manifest should retain only a non-sensitive provenance reference and file checksums.

## 5. File-handling rule on receipt

Before any R1_B event is joined to an MO quote:

1. preserve the delivered files byte-for-byte in a raw local archive;
2. compute SHA-256, byte size, and row count for every data file;
3. record source package/version and acquisition timestamp;
4. inspect schema and a source-random sample only for field mapping and data quality — do not condition the sample on R1_B event timestamps;
5. create a deterministic adapter from source fields to the canonical admission schema;
6. populate `docs/governance/R1B_MO_ADMISSION_MANIFEST_TEMPLATE.json` as a separate actual-source manifest;
7. freeze exchange and broker fee treatment with effective periods;
8. execute `research/r1b_mo_data_admission/validate_mo_quote_source.py`;
9. keep the produced admission receipt even if FAIL.

A failed admission must be fixed only at the source/schema/provenance layer. It must not trigger a change to the R1_B mechanism, strike rule, expiry rule, causal exit, or outcome metric.

## 6. Fail-closed delivery acceptance checklist

The delivery is **not admissible** for the primary mapping if any of the following is true:

- only daily or minute/five-minute OHLC is delivered;
- best bid/ask or their sizes are absent;
- quote timestamps cannot be given exchange-local semantics;
- contract identity/strike/expiry cannot be reconstructed deterministically;
- source provenance is unstable or undocumented;
- files cannot be checksummed and inventoried;
- zero/missing quote semantics are unknown;
- the quote feed cannot distinguish invalid/unavailable quotes sufficiently for ask-entry / bid-exit execution;
- the applicable fee contract remains unfrozen.

No midpoint, last price, close price, theoretical price, or implied-volatility reconstruction can repair a failed primary bid/ask admission after outcomes are seen.
