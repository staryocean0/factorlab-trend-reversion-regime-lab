## Source assessment

### 1. CFFEX official historical data service — preferred source family

CFFEX states that its historical data products cover historical market data generated by listed products/contracts, including snapshot data, and provides four product families: Level-2, Level-1, one-minute and five-minute historical databases.

For this R1_B identity, the correct acquisition target is **official Level-2 historical snapshot data**, not the one-minute/five-minute OHLC database.

Official references:

- https://www.cffex.com.cn/lssjfw/
- https://www.cffex.com.cn/zlxz/
- https://www.cffex.com.cn/zz1000gzqq/

#### Official Level-2 field-capability confirmation — 2026-09-10

CFFEX's own Level-2 market-information authorization guide describes the Level-2 product as five-level market data and explicitly includes:

- contract name / delivery month;
- latest price;
- volume;
- open interest;
- bid prices for the best five levels;
- ask prices for the best five levels;
- bid sizes for the best five levels;
- ask sizes for the best five levels;
- settlement/open/close/high/low/previous-settlement/turnover fields.

The same official guide states that Level-2 uses market **snapshot** sampling at **500 ms per sample** and notes that illiquid distant contracts may not emit a new snapshot if neither trades nor orders changed since the previous sample.

Official field-capability source:

- https://www.cffex.com.cn/u/cms/www/202201/20211342wucd.pdf

This confirms that the official CFFEX Level-2 product family has the top-of-book price/size capability required by the frozen canonical schema. It is **not** sufficient to admit a historical delivery by itself.

Direct retrieval of the CFFEX public acquisition attachments from a GitHub Actions runner was attempted once and failed because the runner had no route to `cffex.com.cn` (`Network is unreachable`). No fake local copy or checksum was created, and the one-off workflow was removed after the failure.
